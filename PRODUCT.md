## Platform

## Stack
Outside Shopify: the user confirmed the new site leaves Shopify (the current site runs on Shopify, theme "OTTOBRE | LAYOUT HOME 2 + QUIZ"). The replacement stack and ecommerce engine (catalogue, cart, checkout, payments, orders) are undecided. Record the choice here before any build starts.

## Users
- Padel players who buy online: rackets and technical apparel (men's and women's SS26), bags, accessories, gift vouchers.
- Padel clubs and sports centres that sell and let players test T91 (about 70 partner centres in Italy and Europe, e.g. GPADEL, Country Padel, Colognola Racquet Club).
- Coaches (programma maestri) and ambassadors (athletes such as Emiliano Iriart and Bubu Salandro).
- Existing owners who need repair, customisation, warranty or customer care.

## Product Purpose
Ecommerce and brand site of T91 Padel ("From Players to players"). The new site must not be only catalogue plus checkout: it has to make the product, the services, the community and the relationships visible. Success is online sales plus awareness and use of the services listed below, which customers do not perceive today.

## Positioning
A padel brand built by players, with a physical network of partner centres where people can find, test and buy the product, and a set of services around the racket (customisation without minimum order, repair of competitor brands, used-racket buy-back, experiences, visible customer care, protection plans). The exact claim that competitors cannot copy still needs the user's confirmation.

## Operating Context
- Current site: https://t91padel.com (Shopify, Italian). Navigation: Shop, Racchette, Ambassador, Buoni regalo, Programma centri, Programma maestri; apparel split Uomo / Donna.
- Current home blocks: "quiz" to pick a racket, new collections (HOLO, Iriart 3.0), racket grid (from €328,90), Mistery Box (levels with listed discounts), SS26 apparel, new arrivals, and a store locator for partner centres with call, WhatsApp and directions actions.
- T91 is a client of NOKNOK, part of the NOKNSG group.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Services the new site must value (client brief, currently not perceived by customers):
- **Personalizzazione**: product customisation, also without a minimum order.
- **T91 Care / Repair**: repair of rackets, including competitor brands.
- **Ritiro usato**: used-racket buy-back; to be evaluated as a dedicated service.
- **T91 Experience**: events, tests, special experiences and occasions to meet the brand.
- **Customer care**: made a visible, distinctive element.
- **Click & Collect**: buy online and collect at a shop or physical point.
- **Garanzie / Protezione**: to be structured and explained with extreme clarity. Formulas given by the client: €109 for a 2-year protection on the purchase; €39,90 for replacement in case of breakage; €59,90 for accidental breakage, fall or chipping. Exact conditions, coverage and terminology must be verified before any public wording.
- **Blog**: news and useful articles, aimed at SEO and at sales.

Also required or existing: racket-choice quiz, partner-centre locator, centres and coaches programmes, ambassador pages, gift vouchers. Italian is the primary language; other languages undecided (the network includes centres in Germany, Switzerland, Croatia and Singapore).

Open decisions: stack and ecommerce engine; whether used-racket buy-back becomes a service; protection plan terms; languages; migration of catalogue, URLs and SEO from Shopify.

- Name: T91 / T91 Padel.
- Voice: the user chose to treat T91 as a NOKNOK client; NOKNOK's voice (Diretto, Curioso, Energico) is the working reference and needs confirming. It never mixes with NSG's voice.
- Visual reference pinned by the user: "sportivo e moda". Fashion-retail discipline from zara.com/it, with the sporting appeal of Nike and Adidas. The current black and orange (HOLO) stays recognisable.
- Existing logo, colours and photography have not been reviewed yet.

## Product Principles
1. Services are products: each one gets a clear, findable place, not a footer line.
2. Clarity on money and coverage: protection plans and repair terms are stated plainly, with nothing implied.
3. Online and physical are one journey: partner centres, click and collect, tests and events connect to the shop.
4. Players speak to players: community and ambassadors give proof, with only real people and real facts.
5. Content that sells: the blog and guides serve search and the buying decision.
