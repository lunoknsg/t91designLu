# Surface brief: home, hub Servizi, Garanzie (prototype)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a player (or owner of a racket) who must discover T91's services, then buy.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The racket is where the relationship starts, not where it ends. Services get the weight of products in a retail grammar; refuses catalogue-plus-checkout with services as a footer line. World pinned by the user: sport and fashion, Zara's retail discipline with Nike/Adidas energy.
OWN-WORLD: White retail ground, black campaign fields, one orange (HOLO) used for action and status. Condensed heavy display caps (Big Shoulders Display) against a neutral grotesk (Archivo), tiny uppercase nav, full-bleed photography, square corners, hairline rules, no cards, no shadows.
STORY: Visitor sees the HOLO racket and understands T91 also repairs, customises, protects, buys back and hosts experiences; believes it through plain prices and the 70-centre network; acts: open a service, buy a racket, find a centre.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim announcement strip, header with T91 wordmark and uppercase nav. Full-bleed black HOLO campaign image; huge headline bottom-left, two actions (Acquista HOLO, Scopri i servizi) under it. Services list peeks at the fold.
FORM: Fashion-retail editorial with sport-brand type scale; services as large typographic rows (not cards). Pinned by the user in chat ("sportivo e moda. per moda mi ispirerei a zara.com/it, ma rimani con appeal sportivo tipo nike o adidas"); a pinned world beats the roll, so no seed key.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
